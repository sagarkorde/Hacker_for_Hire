Labels and rules (from docs/annotation_guideline.md)
1 HAAS_SERVICEThe vendor carries out an intrusion or attack on a target the buyer chooses: hack a named website or account, DDoS or booter/stresser access, database dump to order, doxing.
2 HAAS_TOOLSoftware or infrastructure the buyer runs to attack: RAT, botnet kit, keylogger, crypter, exploit kit, phishing page, cracking software.
3 HAAS_GUIDEInstructions for hacking or intrusion.
4 OTHER_FRAUDFraud goods or guides without intrusion: carding, cash-out, cards, fake documents, scam methods.
5 ACCOUNTS_MEDIAReady-made accounts, subscriptions, gift codes, pay-TV or IPTV, likes and followers, even if the accounts were cracked.
6 OTHERAnything else, or too little text to decide (add ? in the note).

If two labels fit, take the higher one in this list. Judge the offer in the text only. Keys: 1-6 label, arrows move, N next unlabelled.
